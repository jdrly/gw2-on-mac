# 0.1.x is the stable Rosetta line, and 0.2.x is the arm64 line on Mach9

Decided 2026-11-11. Status: accepted. It supersedes my wait of 2026-11-03 for CrossOver's arm64
sources.

0.1.x runs everything under Rosetta: Wine, the graphics driver and the game are x86_64 code that
macOS translates. macOS 28 removes Rosetta for most software. On 2026-11-03 I had ruled out arm64
Wine until CodeWeavers published the sources of CrossOver Preview. Then athei shipped an open arm64
Wine with FEX (Mach9), and a 7-Zip run on it showed FEX at 1.053 times Rosetta's time, not the 40 to
60% slower I remembered. So I split the app into two lines. 0.1.x was stable enough for people to
play, so the arm64 work could start as 0.2.x with the performance review in hand, and I could play
both and compare.

## Consequences

- `main` is 0.1.x on the `cx-26.3.0-8` engine. Nothing of the arm64 engine goes into it until 0.2
  has proved itself in play: Mach9 and the work of my Wine and FEX forks stay on `arm64`. My DXMT
  fork is one branch for both lines, so its work reaches 0.1.x whenever a release bundles a newer
  build. 0.1.3 and 0.1.4 both bundle `e02a4d6`. A 0.1.x release carries fixes, and features of the
  app itself that I order for players. 0.1.4 of 2026-11-16 added ReShade as a Settings switch, fixed
  arcdps's update and crash window, and took the interface addon with its `csspin` setting. It
  changed no engine piece.
- `arm64` is 0.2 on Mach9. Its issues are tracked in #21, and its pull requests go into `arm64`.
- 0.2 installs next to 0.1.x with its own engine, bottle and caches (ADR 0007).
- The order in #21 is: the stock engine, my DXMT fork as ARM64EC, the heat changes ported to Mach9's
  msync, the Mac driver changes, then the app and 0.2.0. Each step was to end in a build I play
  against 0.1.x, and a failed comparison stops the line. I skipped that gate myself for step 2:
  before I had played step 1's build, I asked for a dev build with my DXMT in place of athei's.
- The Wine-side performance issues land on the arm64 tree, not on the x86 one. The DXMT ones are
  engine-independent.
- The same evening I said a 0.2 build without what 0.1.x already has is pointless to test. So work
  isn't paced around partial builds for me to play. The first build I played after that was dev.8,
  with the wake policy, the event pool, the spins, the launcher fix and the mouse setting. That list
  as the meaning of parity is the dispatcher's reading. I didn't enumerate it.
- Since 2026-11-13 the gap in busy places is parked on my word. 0.2 drops below 60 where 0.1.3 holds
  it, the game thread needs 81 to 82 M cycles a frame against 76.7 M available, and FEX and Wine
  fixes from upstream and athei will come. There's no rush.

## Alternatives I rejected

- **Waiting for CrossOver's arm64 sources.** My rule from 2026-11-03 was to wait until CrossOver's
  arm64 Wine sources are public. The dispatcher's note adds that I don't want CrossOver installed
  and that closed sources would block my own Wine fixes. An open engine ended the wait.
- **Moving 0.1.x itself to the arm64 engine.** I don't put untested Wine under a released app.
