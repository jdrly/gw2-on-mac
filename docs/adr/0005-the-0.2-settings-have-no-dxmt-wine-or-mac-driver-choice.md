# The 0.2 Settings have no DXMT, Wine or Mac driver choice

Decided 2026-11-12. Status: accepted. `arm64` only. 0.1.x on `main` keeps its rows.

The first 0.2 builds had a Settings row for each piece of mine: DXMT (my fork or the engine's own),
Wine (my `ntdll.so` or the engine's) and Mac driver (my `winemac.so` or the engine's). After the
first sessions on dev.8 I removed all three. I don't use the engine's own pieces, so the app runs my
DXMT fork, my `ntdll.so` and my `winemac.so` whenever the build carries them.

## Consequences

- `settings_version` goes up, and its step sets `dxmt`, `wine` and `macdrv` back to `fork`, `mine`
  and `mine` once, so no bottle stays on the engine's files with no row left to show it.
- The three keys stay in the backend (`gw2om.sh set dxmt|wine|macdrv …`) for my own comparisons from
  Terminal. Nothing in the window names them.
- The main window's Engine and Graphics checks still say what is installed.
- A row whose reason pointed at one of the removed rows says what is true without naming it.
- `win32u.so` installs with the Mac driver as one set (#130). Before that the app installed no
  `win32u.so`, and its two switches could never act.
- The `wine` setting is still the off switch for my Wine fork's fixes that have none of their own.

## Alternatives I rejected

- **Keeping the choices in the window.** I don't use the engine's own pieces.
