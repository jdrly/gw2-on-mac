## GW2 on Mac 0.1.0

The first version: Guild Wars 2 on Apple silicon, set up in one click through Steam, ArenaNet's own installer or the game files you already have.

### Its own engine and bottle
GW2 on Mac doesn't touch anything you already have. It downloads its own Wine engine (CrossOver
26.3 with Wine 11, from athei's wine-build, checked by its checksum), creates the game's own Wine
bottle with Retina and Game Mode, and installs the game into it: through Steam, with Valve's own
installer, or through ArenaNet's installer, `Gw2Setup-64.exe`, which you download from ArenaNet's
site and drop into the window. GW2 on Mac checks that file's ArenaNet signature first. Every
download of the game comes from Valve or ArenaNet.

### Game files you already have
Already play Guild Wars 2 in CrossOver, Whisky or an app wrapper? Choose *Game files I already
have*: GW2 on Mac finds those installs and brings the game into its own bottle without
downloading it again. On the same disk it is a clone, instant and without taking extra space;
from another disk it is copied. Your graphics settings and remembered login can come along too.
The original stays exactly as it is.

### Steam in the background
After your first sign-in, Play starts Steam without its window and Steam starts the game. You can
turn this off in Settings › Game.

### Direct3D 11 straight to Metal
The game draws through our build of DXMT (`e02a4d6`), tuned for busy maps on Metal 4: fewer
command buffers and `Map` calls per frame, a residency set, and shader warming, which builds the
pipelines your last sessions used before the game asks for them. Its shader cache is GW2 on Mac's
own, in `~/Library/Caches/GW2 on Mac`, apart from any other DXMT setup on your Mac. The game no
longer crashes as it quits: DXMT pins itself in memory, so it is never unloaded while its threads
still run.

The Metal HUD (Settings › Graphics) shows over the game only, not over Steam's windows.

### Tuned for crowds
A tuned Wine for the game's threads: an event pool for its job groups, single wakes for its job
workers, short spins before sleeps on busy locks, and three of its job workers on the cooler
cores. Settings › Performance turns each of them off, and sets how many CPUs the game sees, from 2
up to all of your Mac's.

### Sharp text at 4K
Settings › Interface can make the game's Interface Size "Larger" larger, from the game's own
crisp fonts. It is off by default and asks first: it changes the game's memory, which ArenaNet
doesn't support, and may stop working after a game update.

### Yours, and reversible
Uninstall removes everything GW2 on Mac created: the engine, the bottle with the game in it, and
its caches, DXMT's shader cache with them. *Collect Logs…* makes a zip for bug reports without account names or passwords.
