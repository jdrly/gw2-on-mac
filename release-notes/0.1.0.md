## GW2 on Mac 0.1.0

The first version: Guild Wars 2 on Apple silicon, set up in one click through Steam or ArenaNet's own installer.

### Its own engine and bottle
GW2 on Mac doesn't touch anything you already have. It downloads its own Wine engine (CrossOver
26.3 with Wine 11, from athei's wine-build, checked by its checksum), creates the game's own Wine
bottle with Retina and Game Mode, and installs the game into it: through Steam, with Valve's own
installer, or through ArenaNet's installer, `Gw2Setup-64.exe`, which you download from ArenaNet's
site and drop into the window. GW2 on Mac checks that file's ArenaNet signature first. Every
download of the game comes from Valve or ArenaNet.

### Direct3D 11 straight to Metal
The game draws through our build of DXMT (`684f428`), tuned for busy maps on Metal 4: fewer
command buffers and `Map` calls per frame, a residency set, and shader warming, which builds the
pipelines your last sessions used before the game asks for them.

### Tuned for crowds
A tuned Wine for the game's threads: an event pool for its job groups, single wakes for its job
workers, short spins before sleeps on busy locks, and three of its job workers on the cooler
cores. Settings › Performance turns each of them off.

### Sharp text at 4K
Settings › Interface can make the game's Interface Size "Larger" larger, from the game's own
crisp fonts. It is off by default and asks first: it changes the game's memory, which ArenaNet
doesn't support, and may stop working after a game update.

### Yours, and reversible
Uninstall removes everything GW2 on Mac created: the engine, the bottle with the game in it, and
its caches. *Collect Logs…* makes a zip for bug reports without account names or passwords.

### Support

you use it at your own risk. Please send questions and bug reports to Pharaphobia on

never to ArenaNet's support.
