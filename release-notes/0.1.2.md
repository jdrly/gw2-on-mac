## GW2 on Mac 0.1.2

The window now has a Discord button that opens the server where I help.

### Discord in the window
The top of the window has a *Discord* button next to *Unofficial fan project*. It opens the
Discord server where I answer questions about GW2 on Mac. Bring your questions about arcdps and
other addons there too, and your bug reports. For a bug, attach the zip from *Collect Logs…*.

### Support
GW2 on Mac is an unofficial project. ArenaNet and NCSOFT don't make, endorse or support it, and
you use it at your own risk. Please send questions and bug reports to me, Pharaphobia, on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/AnV3fZWz2b) or as a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jdrly/gw2-on-mac/issues),
never to ArenaNet's support.
