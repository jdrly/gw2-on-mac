## GW2 on Mac 0.1.4

ReShade is now a switch in Settings, off by default, the way arcdps is.

### ReShade in Settings › Interface
ReShade is crosire's post-processing program. It draws effects such as sharpening, colour
correction or anti-aliasing over the finished picture. The new *ReShade* switch under arcdps asks
first, then downloads ReShade 6.8.0 from reshade.me and checks it against the sha256 I pinned. It
puts ReShade next to `Gw2-64.exe` as `d3d11.dll`, with Microsoft's shader compiler
`d3dcompiler_47.dll`. That's the game's own copy from `bin64/cef` if it has one, and otherwise a
copy from mozilla/fxc2, pinned the same way. If you haven't added shaders of your own yet, turning
ReShade on for the first time also downloads two effect packs from GitHub, ReShade's standard
effects and SweetFX. Turning it off removes what the app put
into the game folder.

ReShade isn't made or supported by ArenaNet, NCSOFT or me, and you use it at your own risk. The
README quotes ArenaNet's policy on third-party programs.

### Your presets and shaders stay outside the bottle
ReShade's settings, log, presets, shaders and screenshots live in
`~/Library/Application Support/GW2 on Mac/ReShade`. *Open ReShade folder* in the row shows it.
Turning ReShade off, Repair and Uninstall leave that folder where it is. *Reset the shader cache*
now empties ReShade's cache too.

### Presets and shaders by drag and drop
Drop a preset, a zip or a folder on the Settings window, or use *Add preset or shaders…* in the
ReShade row. Presets go to `Presets`, and the contents of `Shaders` and `Textures` folders go to
folders named after the zip or folder. Nothing else comes in, so no `.dll`, `.addon64` or `.exe`.
The row then says what went where and which effects a preset needs that aren't there. When one
preset came in and the game is closed, the app asks whether ReShade should start with it.

### The menu, and what doesn't work
ReShade's menu key is Home. On a MacBook that's fn + Left Arrow, and the README says how to pick
another key. The app uses ReShade's signed build. When it sees high network activity, it turns its
add-ons off, so effects that read the depth buffer, such as ambient occlusion and depth of field,
get no depth buffer then. I haven't checked yet how often that happens in Guild Wars 2.

### arcdps and ReShade together
Both switches can be on at once. Then ReShade is `d3d11.dll` next to `Gw2-64.exe`, and it loads
arcdps itself from `addons\arcdps\chain\d3d11.dll`, through two keys the app sets in ReShade's own
`ReShade.ini`.

### arcdps's update and its crash window
On 0.1.3, arcdps's update came back with the same version after the restart, and its crash window
never opened. arcdps does both in a second process, and that process got Wine's own `d3d11.dll`
instead of arcdps's. 0.1.4 gives it arcdps's. I can't show the update working before arcdps next
updates.

### What I tested
I played two sessions with ReShade at 4K. In the first, only ReShade loaded. Its log is complete,
and it compiled the effects of both packs with the game's own `d3dcompiler_47.dll`. I turned on
Levels and the picture changed, and the game looked nicer for it. *Larger interface* was on too.
In the second, ReShade's menu and arcdps's options window were open in the game at the same time.
I haven't tested thirty Cmd+Tabs, the frame rate against ReShade off, the preset drop in the real
window, or an arcdps self-update with ReShade loading it. I tested the app's side in a sandbox with
stand-in files: the switches, the folder, the import, ReShade loading arcdps and Collect Logs.

ReShade's installer, its `ReShade64.dll` and fxc2's compiler are pinned to the sha256 of the files
reshade.me and mozilla/fxc2 served when I checked them.

### Collect Logs knows ReShade
The zip now has ReShade's log, its `ReShade.ini`, the current preset and the names of the files
under `Shaders`. `system-info.txt` and the session report say whether ReShade and arcdps were in.

### Critical sections can spin first
My interface addon has a new setting, `csspin` in the game folder's `gw2-ui-scale.ini`. With
`csspin=5`, the game's critical sections spin for 5 microseconds before they sleep. It's off by
default and works only with *Larger interface* on.

### The session report's compile waits
The session report multiplied DXMT's count of shader compile waits by their total, so its sum came
out too high. It now adds the totals as the log gives them, and `--compare` shows the longest wait
too.
