## GW2 on Mac arm64 0.2.0-dev.2 (development build)

The game draws with my DXMT fork on the arm64 engine, so I can compare it with 0.1.x and with the
engine's own DXMT.

### My DXMT fork, built for this engine
0.2.0-dev.1 drew with the DXMT that Mach9's engine ships (athei/dxmt `d903db8`). This build puts
my fork in its place, built for the engine: Metal 4, the residency set, shader warming and the
STATS lines. The engine's own DXMT stays next to it, unchanged, and nothing inside the engine's
`wine.app` is touched. Steam's windows use the same DXMT as the game, as on 0.1.x.

### A setting to compare the two
*Settings › Graphics › DXMT* switches between my fork and the engine's own. The switch happens
while the game and Steam are closed, otherwise at the next start after Steam has quit. Each keeps
its own shader cache, so switching never hands one the other's.

### Settings that work again
- *Shader warming* and *MetalFX for Subsample* are back. They need my fork, so they grey out
  while the engine's own DXMT is chosen.
- With *Debug logging* on, DXMT's log has the STATS lines again, and the session report reads
  them and names the DXMT that ran.
- Cooler job workers, faster locks, raw mouse-look and Game Mode still wait for my Wine changes.

### After updating
Open GW2 on Mac arm64 with the game and Steam closed and click *Repair*, or just press Play. Either
puts my DXMT into the engine, and the window's *Graphics* line names its commit.
